## **Part 2**
### **Real-world examples / Analogies**
- **Bus:** Think of a bus. It's designed to transport a number of people. When the bus gets overloaded, more busses may be dispatched to help transport more people. When demand goes down, some busses may go back to the depot to reduce the amount of resources that are being used The people in this instance would be data, and the memory allocation would be the busses
- **Buying tickets:** Imagine buying tickets to see your favorite artist. It may be extremely difficult to purchase due to limited seating. Syncing up the ticket buying availability is cruicial to ensuring everybody has an equal chance of purchasing their tickets. If it isn't synced up properly, one person may get a much better opportunity to purchase tickets than another concert-goer. Here, the display would be the availability of ticket purchasing, and syncing them up is what is important.
